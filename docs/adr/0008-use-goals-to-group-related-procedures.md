# Use Goals to group related Procedures

A Goal is a stable, bilingual organizational grouping of closely related Procedures around a broad administrative objective, such as getting a passport, obtaining a National ID, or handling military paperwork. It is the user-entry point and keeps a large catalog of government transactions navigable; it is not itself a government transaction and does not own Procedure-specific rules, requirements, fees, steps, routing, or evidence.

The planning operation receives a Goal and Facts, considers that Goal's curated candidate Procedures, and resolves the concrete Procedure that fits the person's circumstances. For example, one passport Goal may contain first issuance, ordinary renewal, lost-passport replacement, and damaged-passport replacement as distinct Procedures. This deliberately allows Goal wording to remain broad and stable while the Procedure catalog grows and administrative routes differ.

This decision supersedes earlier wording that made Goals resemble concrete Procedure names such as “renew a passport.”
