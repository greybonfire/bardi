## Editorial consequences

The assistant may explain a researched administrative process and match an applicant's reported
administrative records to it. It must not predict whether depression, anxiety, a neurological
condition, a physical impairment, a measurement or disability-card possession will produce a
medical exemption. The research did not establish a universal accepted-diagnosis list.

C06 is a narrow historical documentary provision, not advice to obtain hospital admission,
alter treatment, or present a particular symptom. Current certification scope needs legal and
clinical review before it becomes a checklist item. Do not turn stigmatizing language in S02
into Bardi's clinical categories, identity questions, or an assertion that sexual orientation is
an illness. Preserve the exact source locator for reviewers and use respectful public wording.

The medical decision, exemption record, physical certificate, and certificate-service eligibility
are separate. A reported decision is a source Fact, not verification by Bardi of an official
database. A pending referral does not establish an exemption; a missing certificate does not
establish lack of entitlement. No assumed certificate expiry, renewal cycle, travel permission,
employment restriction or effect on civil capacity was established.

case circumstances; they cannot establish a tariff, accepted evidence alternative or authority rule.
