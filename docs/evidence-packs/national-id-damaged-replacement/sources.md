## Boundaries that remain unresolved

- **D03:** marriage-document applicability, including unchanged recorded marriage.
  A `none` data-change answer does not prove marriage proof unnecessary.
- **G02:** complete mandatory checklist and channel entitlement; forms, photographs,
  copies, quantities, fees, steps, office and turnaround remain unknown. Do not borrow
  renewal, lost-card or consular requirements.
- **G07:** damage combined with expiry or changes, transaction precedence and charges.
  SC34 uses an expired damaged card but is an **unknown, unexecuted research specification**,
  not authority for a positive combined-case plan. [scenarios]
- **D09:** later amendments, regulations and exceptions have not been reviewed into a
  current-law conclusion. The suite's Article 48 amendment research does not certify
  current Article 54. [claims]

Domestic application, retained damaged card, no requested changes, Egyptian citizenship
and strictly future expiry bound this product research case. They are not a complete
statutory eligibility analysis; excluded combinations are not declared legally ineligible.
September 22 coverage is authored, not a government effective date. Exact jurisdiction
and expired-and-damaged selection are not established. [catalog] [facts]

An unconditional important product Warning states that guidance is incomplete, an empty
checklist **does not mean no requirements**, and rechecking is necessary. Unverified
administrative evidence may be absent while that warning remains. No source dates or trust
are advanced for persistent publication. See the [operations handoff](../../operations/national-id-damaged-replacement.md)
for files, setup, measured tests and remaining editorial blockers; broader guidance needs documentary
and channel clarification, not a user answer substituting for government policy. [implementation]
