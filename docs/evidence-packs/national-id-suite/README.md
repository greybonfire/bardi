Research snapshot: **2026-09-12**. Documentation split: **2026-09-14**.

This pack owns **15 National ID families (N01–N15)**: domestic first issuance, unchanged renewal,
loss, damage, residence/profession/marital changes, record disputes, combined cases, early-renewal
gaps, and mission/committee-scoped overseas services. MFA guidance plus London, Dubai and Sydney
examples do not establish permanent worldwide availability or complete domestic checklists.

The [passport suite](../passport-suite/README.md) remains separate. A passport's ID-document
prerequisite does not turn ID replacement or data change into passport renewal. Current-law,
lost-card documentary, marriage-proof and live committee gaps remain publication blockers.
No family is publication-ready merely because it has a catalog row.

## Evidence and review boundaries

Evidence retrieval remains **2026-09-12**; no sources were re-fetched for this documentation split.
Undated publication/effective dates remain unknown. A copyright year or retrieval date is not
an administrative commencement date. `supported` describes scoped inspected text, not human
approval or proof of a live service. Fees, office availability and mission instructions require
fresh verification before an authoritative draft is imported.

Accountable human author, independent bilingual reviewer, and applicable legal/military/
custody specialists remain **unassigned**. No contacts, applications, payments, appointments,
personal case collection, production imports or publication approvals were performed by this PR.
Missing government policy must be researched, not answered by applicants as a legal conclusion.

The [production architecture](../../architecture/README.md), accepted ADRs, and
[editorial process](../../editorial-process.md) remain authoritative. Proposed IDs and Facts are
not live database/API additions. The shared [I02 prerequisite](../catalog-compatibility.md)
