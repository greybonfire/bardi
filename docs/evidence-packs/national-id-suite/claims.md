# National ID claim ledger, discrepancies and gaps

This ledger owns the administrative claims for this family. IDs retain the 2026-09-12 research
meaning; related procedure/scenario files refer here. Each assertion needs its own scoped evidence.
Alternatives are not permission to require all alternatives at once. `supported` means inspected
text supports the stated scope, subject to independent review; `review` denotes unresolved
interpretation/applicability and `lead` is insufficient for publication. Undated claims retain
unknown effective-from; retrieval dates never become administrative dates by default.

Do not derive penalties from deadlines. Current penalty amounts, exceptions, domestic fees and
the complete amended law/regulations were not established. Reviewed month-based deadlines use
calendar months, not fixed 30-day periods; a late applicant is not automatically ineligible.

A simultaneous missing passport/ID needs the [passport dependency boundary](../passport-suite/procedures.md),
not a merged suite or a circular prerequisite. Shared gap G01 concerns this family's charges;
passport insurance is not an ID requirement. Shared G05 applies only to genuine cross-document cases.
