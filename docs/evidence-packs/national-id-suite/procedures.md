# National ID procedure-family catalog

Catalog row IDs are research references. Proposed Procedure IDs are not live records.
Rows without an identity leave transaction modeling unresolved; do not make every reason or
channel a separate Procedure. Use [claims](claims.md) and [Facts](facts-and-questions.md).
`draftable` means a bounded draft with explicit gaps is possible; `blocked` means consequential
details/selection remain unestablished. Neither status permits publication.

## Version contents and conditions

| Component | What can be authored | What remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selection | Issuance, physical loss/damage, unchanged renewal and genuine changes from source circumstances | G07 combined cases; N08 record disputes |
| Checklist | C-N-06–09 within channel/transaction scope | Proof alternatives, universal photo/copy quantities, D02/D03 |
| Steps | Application/deadline obligations after legal review | Domestic purchase/capture/attendance/payment/collection sequence |
| Fees | Explicit unknown domestic amounts and separate scoped overseas research | Current domestic tiers, same-day price, penalties/exemptions and totals |
| Dependencies | Authoritative records before asserting they can be copied into a card | Foreign civil-event registration and accepted court/registry processes |
| Routing | C-N-03 statutory framework and scoped service-center/mission leads | Complete territory-office mapping and live channels |
| Warnings | Reviewed deadlines/old-card-use rules; separate product limits | No calculated fine or denial merely because a deadline passed |

An alternative proof route remains an alternative; do not require every proof or invent an
Eligibility Basis to control interview order. A held card with one genuine change belongs to
its reviewed change family regardless of expiry, but activation must await confirmed transaction
semantics. Loss/damage plus changes or several changes stay G07, not guessed precedence or multiple
charges. N08 disputed/underlying-record corrections are not ordinary counter updates.

## Channels and adjacent cases

Student, union, public/private employment, pensioner and unemployed cases need scoped evidence;
London/Dubai documents do not establish domestic proof menus. Foreign civil events, nationality,
contested identity and custody are G08 specialist boundaries. Visiting first-ID committees and
ordinary consular renewal counters remain distinct; Sydney's unverified dates do not promise a
permanent first-issuance service. Lost/damaged overseas cards are not covered merely because a
renewal page exists.

Both passport and ID unavailable requires a researched proof/dependency route under G05, linked
only where needed to the [passport catalog](../passport-suite/procedures.md). Do not create a
cycle or a passport-suite dependency for every ID application. Recovery of a replaced ID remains
subject to C-N-04 after current-law review; do not infer passport usability from it.

Accessible/mobile/home/hospital/postal/online services are channel/Service Point material unless
the transaction differs. G06/G10 govern availability; a portal entry establishes neither online
eligibility nor end-to-end completion. Urgency and lateness do not remove documentary obligations.

## Per-version publication checklist

Each version needs a named human author, reviewed bilingual text, explicit applicability and
editorial interval, correctly scoped evidence for every material claim, resolved consequential
discrepancies and passing production Planning Scenarios. Legal interpretation and applicable
military/custody/guardianship/contested-identity branches need the relevant independent specialists.
Fees or exact routing may remain unknown where permitted; missing transaction definitions or
required-document alternatives cannot be hidden inside a supposedly complete plan.
