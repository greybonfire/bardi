# Claim ledger, discrepancies, and gaps

This is the canonical claim text for this research pack. Procedure cards and scenarios refer to
these IDs instead of creating independent copies. Each row must become separate evidence-bearing
material as appropriate; a row containing alternatives is not permission to require them all.

`supported` means inspected official text supports the stated scope on 2026-09-12, subject to
independent review. `review` means the text was inspected but interpretation/current applicability
is unresolved. `lead` is not sufficient for a public claim. Undated web claims have unknown
administrative effective-from; do not set it to the retrieval date. Published version dates are
separate editorial decisions.

Do not derive penalties from these deadlines. Current penalty amounts, exceptions, fees, and
the complete amended law/regulations were not established. Calendar arithmetic, once legally

not automatically ineligible to apply.

Unknown facts and missing research are different. Applicant answers can resolve circumstances
such as loss location. They cannot establish G01's fee, G03's accepted evidence menu, or G06's
jurisdiction rule. Keep local uncertainty local where the production contract permits it; if
the transaction itself cannot be determined safely, the outcome remains inconclusive.
