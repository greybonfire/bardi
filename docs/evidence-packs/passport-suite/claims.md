# Passport claim ledger, discrepancies and gaps

This ledger owns the administrative claims for this family. IDs retain the 2026-09-12 research
meaning; related procedure/scenario files refer here. Each assertion needs its own scoped evidence.
Alternatives are not permission to require all alternatives at once. `supported` means inspected
text supports the stated scope, subject to independent review; `review` denotes unresolved
interpretation/applicability and `lead` is insufficient for publication. Undated claims retain
unknown effective-from; retrieval dates never become administrative dates by default.

Fee rules use C-P-16 **or** C-P-17 according to the transaction and at most one chosen accelerated
component. This does not establish an insurance charge, other surcharge, every payment method
or an all-in total. A published timing statement does not guarantee a case awaiting approval
or loss investigation will meet it. Exceptional military status and documentary sufficiency
need specialist interpretation; newborn return documents and ordinary passports remain distinct.

The ID prerequisite links to the [separate National ID ledger](../national-id-suite/claims.md),
including current-law gate [D09](../national-id-suite/claims.md#d09). It does not direct every
missing-ID case to unchanged renewal. Adult consular first-issuance details remain unresearched
under the mission/specialist gaps G06/G08; National ID committee gap G09 is not its substitute.
