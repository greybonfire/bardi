# Temporary family exemption Sources and Evidence Links

Research snapshot: 2026-08-26

This file preserves claim-level provenance for `temporary_family_exemption_from_military_service`. Because these are consequential legal/military-status claims, every statutory Basis remains subject to specialist review even when the underlying text is current-as-researched.

Exact source fragments are deliberately short anchors. Reviewers must inspect the cited article/page and current consolidated law rather than treating a detached fragment as the complete legal rule.
